Text Message Terms

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Program

ABC Aloha Crew Review Requests. ABC Aloha Crew LLC is a handyman service in Honolulu, Hawaii. After we finish a job for you, we may text you to ask how it went and invite you to leave a Google review.

How you sign up

You join when you give us your mobile number while booking a job and we let you know we will text you after the job. You do not have to agree to texts to hire us.

How often

Up to 2 messages per job: one review request after the job and at most one reminder a few days later.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. ABC Aloha Crew does not charge for these texts.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop all messages. You will get one reply confirming you are unsubscribed, and no more texts after that. Reply START to join again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (808) 582-8482 or abcalohacrew@gmail.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your mobile number. See our privacy policy for details.
